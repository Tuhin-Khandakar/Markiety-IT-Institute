# Course Materials Folder

## 📚 Upload Your Course Files Here

This folder contains downloadable course materials for students.

### Quick Start:

1. **For Digital Marketing Course:**
   - Upload your PowerPoint as: `digital-marketing-course.pptx`
   
2. **For Other Courses:**
   - Graphics Design: `graphics-design-course.pptx`
   - Video Editing: `video-editing-course.pptx`
   - Basic Computer: `basic-computer-course.pptx`
   - Freelancing: `freelancing-course.pptx`

### Current Files:
- `digital-marketing-slides.md` - Placeholder (replace with your .pptx file)

### Supported Formats:
- PowerPoint: `.pptx`, `.ppt`
- PDF: `.pdf`
- Word: `.docx`, `.doc`
- Excel: `.xlsx`, `.xls`
- Videos: `.mp4`, `.webm`

### How Students Access:
1. Student logs into portal (`/students/portal.html`)
2. Sees "Slides & Sheets" section
3. Downloads materials for their enrolled course

### File Naming Convention:
Use lowercase with hyphens:
- ✅ `digital-marketing-course.pptx`
- ✅ `week-1-introduction.pdf`
- ❌ `Digital Marketing Course.pptx`
- ❌ `Week 1 Introduction.pdf`
