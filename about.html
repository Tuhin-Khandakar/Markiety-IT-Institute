About Markiety IT Institute (MIT)

A future-focused learning hub nurturing skilled freelancers, agency specialists, and digital entrepreneurs under the Markiety umbrella.

Our Story

Founded by Md. Tuhin Khandakar, Markiety IT Institute (MIT) began as an internal training program for Markiety. The initiative quickly grew into a full-fledged institute as aspiring freelancers and graduates sought industry-relevant guidance.

Today, MIT combines Markiety's agency expertise with a practical curriculum tailored for the Bangladeshi market, helping learners translate skills into income.

At a Glance

  • 5 core programs with modular learning paths
  • Hybrid classes and recorded sessions
  • Career mentorship & community support
  • Hands-on freelancing playbooks

Mission

To equip Bangladeshi youth with market-ready digital skills and the confidence to monetize their talent globally.

Vision

To become the most trusted tech education brand in Bangladesh, producing freelancers, founders, and professionals who drive economic growth.

Founder's Message

"Education must be practical, affordable, and outcome-driven."

I started MIT to solve a real problem: talented youth lacked mentorship and a roadmap to earn. Our mission is simple — help learners acquire skills, build real projects, and find earning opportunities fast.

We uphold Markiety's values of professionalism, empathy, and relentless experimentation. Join us and let's build a career you are proud of.
