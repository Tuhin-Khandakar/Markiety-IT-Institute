1. Policy Overview

Markiety IT Institute (MIT) is committed to being fair and transparent with our refund policy. We understand that circumstances can change, and we want to ensure that every student feels confident when enrolling in our programs. This policy outlines when and how refunds are processed.

Our refund tiers are designed to be straightforward and predictable. The amount you receive back depends on how much of the course has already been delivered when you request the refund.

2. Refund Tiers

100%

Full Refund — Before Course Start

If you cancel your enrollment at least 7 days before the official course start date, you will receive a full refund of the total course fee paid, subject to a BDT 500 processing fee.

50%

Partial Refund — Within 7 Days of Start

If you cancel within 7 days of the course start date (but before attending more than 2 sessions), you will receive a 50% refund of the total course fee paid.

0%

No Refund — After 7 Days or 2+ Sessions

No refund will be issued after 7 days from the course start date or after the student has attended 2 or more live sessions, whichever comes first. This is because we begin allocating instructor time, batch resources, and platform access from day one.

Note: If MIT cancels a program before it begins for any reason (e.g., insufficient enrollment, instructor unavailability), students will receive a full refund or the option to transfer to the next available batch at no extra cost.

3. Eligibility Criteria

To be eligible for a refund, you must:

  • Submit a written cancellation request via email to markietyitinstitute@gmail.com with the subject line "Refund Request";
  • Include your full name, enrollment/tracking number, course name, and reason for cancellation in the email;
  • Ensure the request is submitted within the applicable refund window (see Section 2);
  • Provide proof of payment (transaction ID, receipt, or screenshot) if not already on file;
  • Return any physical course materials or access credentials provided by MIT (if applicable).

Requests that do not include all required information may be delayed until complete details are provided.

4. How to Request a Refund

Email Us Your Request

Send an email to markietyitinstitute@gmail.com with subject "Refund Request". Include your name, tracking/enrollment number, course name, and reason for cancellation.

Wait for Review (1–3 Business Days)

Our admin team will review your request, verify your enrollment and attendance records, and determine your refund eligibility.

Receive Approval & Refund Method Confirmation

We will email you a refund approval notice with the refund amount and ask for your preferred refund method (bKash, Nagad, Rocket, or bank transfer). Note: refunds are sent to the original payment number or an account in the enrolled student's name.

Refund Processed (within 15 Business Days)

The refund will be sent to your nominated account within 15 business days of confirming your details. You will receive a transaction confirmation once the refund is sent.

5. Processing Timeline

  • Review period: 1–3 business days after we receive your request with complete information.
  • Refund delivery: Within 15 business days after approval and account confirmation.
  • Method: Refunds are sent via the same payment method used for enrollment (bKash, Nagad, Rocket, or bank transfer). If the original method is unavailable, an alternative can be arranged by mutual agreement.

If your refund is taking longer than 15 business days after approval, please contact us and we will investigate with the payment provider.

6. Exceptional Circumstances

Special Cases Considered on Merit

In the following circumstances, MIT may consider a refund outside the standard tiers on a case-by-case basis, even after the standard refund window has closed:

  • Medical emergency: Serious illness or injury affecting the student's ability to continue (requires medical documentation such as a doctor-issued certificate).
  • Natural disaster or emergency: Floods, fires, or other major events preventing the student from attending (requires reasonable evidence).
  • Relocation: Unexpected permanent relocation outside Bangladesh that makes continuing impractical.
  • Course material misrepresentation: If the course content or outcomes differ materially from what was advertised at the time of enrollment (verified by MIT).

To request consideration under exceptional circumstances, email markietyitinstitute@gmail.com with documentation supporting your case. Each request is reviewed individually, and our decision is final.
