Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about MIT courses

What courses does MIT offer?

We offer 5 industry-focused courses: Digital Marketing, Graphics Design, Video Editing, Basic Computer Course, and Freelancing & Career Development. Each course is designed to make you job-ready in 2-3 months.

Are the certificates government-approved?

Yes! All MIT certificates are government-approved and recognized by employers and agencies across Bangladesh and internationally for freelance platforms.

How do I access course materials after payment?

After admission, you'll receive a portal login (phone number + 4-digit PIN) via SMS. Log in to the Student Portal to access slides, homework, and resources 24/7.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bKash, Cash, and Nagad. For bKash payments, you'll receive account details during admission. You can also pay in installments by contacting our support team.

Do you offer career support after graduation?

Absolutely! We provide freelance profile reviews, interview prep, and client pitching strategies. Many of our graduates get their first clients within weeks of completing the course.

Can I switch courses after enrollment?

Yes, you can switch to another course by contacting the admin. Course fees may be adjusted based on the pricing difference.

How long does each course take?

Most courses run for 2-3 months with weekly live sessions and self-paced modules. Basic Computer Course is 2.5 months, while Digital Marketing, Graphics Design, and Video Editing are 3 months each.

Is there a discount for early enrollment?

Yes! Many courses offer limited-time discounts with seat limits. Check the "Courses" page to see current discount availability and remaining seats.
